This mod is intended for players who use keep inventory and the mentally disturbed (same group am I right hur hur hur).__
On respawn there is a small chance that your inventory's contents will be wiped, even with keep inventory on.__
This chance can be moddified in the common config.__
This mod was specially for the mod pack The Book of John, which you can find at https://github.com/SourpussMaggie/Book-Of-John
